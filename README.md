# 🛍️ Flipkart — E-Commerce UI/UX Concept

> **Designing a familiar shopping experience with a focus on simplicity, usability, and smooth navigation.**

A **UI/UX design project created in Figma**, inspired by the Flipkart e-commerce experience.

---

## 💡 The Idea Behind the Design

A good shopping experience is not only about how an interface looks — it is also about how easily users can find what they need.

> **From finding a product to completing a purchase — every interaction matters.**
---

## ✨ What I Designed

* 🏠 E-commerce home screen
* 🔎 Product browsing experience
* 📦 Product catalogue
* 🛒 Shopping cart
* 💳 Checkout flow
* 🧭 User navigation
* 📱 Mobile-focused interface design
  
---

### 🎯 Figma

### 🧩 UI Design

### 👤 UX Design

---

## 🔄 User Journey

The designed experience follows a simple shopping flow:

**Home → Browse Products → Select Product → Add to Cart → Checkout**

The goal was to keep each step clear and reduce unnecessary complexity in the shopping journey.
